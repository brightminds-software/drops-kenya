Premium DJ drops and audio branding from Nairobi, Kenya.  
We specialize in custom voice branding, WhatsApp ordering, and professional audio drops for DJs, radio stations, clubs, and events across Kenya and beyond.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Mobile-First & Responsive:** Optimized for all devices, from mobile phones to large desktop screens.
- **Advanced SEO Optimization:** 
  - Dynamic Canonical URLs and Open Graph/Twitter Card meta tags.
  - JSON-LD Schema markup (Organization, WebSite, and BlogPosting).
  - Auto-generated `sitemap.xml` and optimized `robots.txt`.
- **Seamless Domain Migration:** Configured `vercel.json` for permanent (301) redirects from the legacy `.vercel.app` domain to the new custom domain.
- **Interactive UI/UX:** 
  - Custom share button with native mobile Web Share API integration.
  - Scroll-triggered animations and progress indicators.
  - Custom audio players with visual equalizer effects.
- **Direct WhatsApp Integration:** One-click ordering and inquiry system routed directly to our business WhatsApp.
- **Modular Blog System:** Educative, SEO-friendly articles to drive organic traffic and establish industry authority.
