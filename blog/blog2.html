Understanding Music Rights in Kenya

Published February 2026 • By Drops-Kenya

Music rights protect your creative work and ensure you earn from it. In Kenya, artists often lose income because they don’t understand how royalties and copyright work.

Key Concepts

  • Copyright: Grants you ownership of your music and prevents unauthorized use.
  • Royalties: Payments you receive when your music is played, streamed, or broadcast.
  • Collective Management: Organizations like MCSK help collect royalties on behalf of artists.

By registering your work and joining a collective management organization, you protect your music and secure your earnings.
