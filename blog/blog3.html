Marketing Yourself as a DJ

Published February 2026 • By Drops-Kenya

Being a great DJ is not enough — you need to market yourself to grow your audience and bookings. Social media and live events are powerful tools.

Strategies to Try

  • TikTok: Share short mixes, drops, and behind‑the‑scenes clips.
  • Instagram: Post event highlights, flyers, and interact with fans.
  • Live Events: Network with promoters and collaborate with other DJs.
  • Branding: Use consistent visuals, logos, and DJ drops to stand out.

With consistent marketing, you’ll build a loyal fan base and attract more opportunities.
